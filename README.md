📌 Project: Bike Store Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

Overview:
This project analyzes sales performance of a bike store chain using Power BI. It includes insights into revenue trends, customer distribution, product performance, and brand/store contributions.

Key Insights:

Total Revenue: 7.69M

Total Customers: 1,445

Yearly Trend:
Revenue trend show peak in 2017 (3.3 M ) and then decline in 2018 (1.5 M)

Revenue Comparision between 2016 and 2017 (by month
shows a 29.86% increase)

Decline in 2018 (~50%)

Brand Contribution: Trek leads with ~60% of total revenue

Category Contribution: Mountain Bikes (1.85M), Road Bikes (1.59M) are top performers

City/Store Analysis: Baldwin city & store are highest revenue generators (~1.09M)

Product Insights: Trek Slash 8 27.5, Trek Conduit+, and Trek Fuel EX 8 29 are top-selling products

Monthly Pattern: Peak in April, decline in May, slight upward trend June–December
